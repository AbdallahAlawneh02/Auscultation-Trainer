Welcome to the Heart and Lung Sounds Trainer System.

About the Platform

This advanced medical simulation platform utilizes an RFID-embedded training vest and a smart stethoscope to train medical students in realistic auscultation. The system evaluates the ability to locate anatomical spots and diagnose normal vs. abnormal sounds.

How to run an Exam

  • 1
    Instructor Setup: Go to the setup tab and assign 10 points.
  • 2
    Launch: Click "Launch Exam" to lock the interface.
  • 3
    Scan: Student finds the location with the smart stethoscope.
  • 4
    Diagnose: Listen and answer. Auto-scoring occurs instantly.
